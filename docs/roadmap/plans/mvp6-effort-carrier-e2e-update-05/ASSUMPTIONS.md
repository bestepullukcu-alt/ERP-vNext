# Assumptions and boundaries

1. Update-04's fixed 103-row rubric and O/M/P denominator are retained.
2. The reported percentage is **estimated effort completion**. It is not a readiness percentage, schedule percentage, production-readiness score or acceptance percentage.
3. Browser acceptance rows are assigned only to Carrier Frontend. Runtime/infrastructure acceptance rows are assigned only to Carrier Integration. No acceptance row receives two numeric credits.
4. Shared Auth implementation and Auth-chain verification remain exclusively under `SHARED`; Carrier consumes the proven seam without repeating those hours.
5. The Carrier Test/VER remaining row combines bounded E2E, PNG, CT and final acceptance. The evidence provides no reliable hour split, so no numeric Test/VER credit is invented.
6. Durable PNG, CT disposition, common publication, full-module acceptance, E5/G5 and rollout remain outside completed scope.
7. Shipment integration rework remains prepared but unapplied, with Update-04 estimates and reserve allocation unchanged.
