### Exact error order proposed for owner decision

For a mutation, check JWT/permission first, then server-derived tenant/legal-entity and required header/schema validation, then scoped target existence (cross-scope is 404), then receipt identity/fingerprint. A matching receipt replays before current mutable state or fixture revalidation. A mismatching receipt is the proposed amended 409. A new key then checks active/state conflicts, then exact fixture/source validity, then commits aggregate/receipt/audit/outbox as applicable. A stale executor version/lease is an internal CAS miss and retry/reload, not an invented client error. The contract lacks a named invalid-constraint response and payload-conflict response; neither may be presented as published behavior. `EVALUATION_ALREADY_ACTIVE` applies only to a different key against an active scenario.
