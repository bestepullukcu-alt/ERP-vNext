2. **Assignment (recorded, O-Q2-3).** MOD-0023 resolves ONE assignee from the candidate list (`snapshot.ResolvedPrincipalId`):
   with [A, B] it took B; the starter's attempt is refused 403 before SoD. With [A] only, A is the assignee and the decision can
   only be refused (409) — it waits until timeout / withdraw.

   Inventory (Kestrel) accepted it. Real MDM is Kestrel; recorded for the fleet, not a Q2 code issue.
