**reviseScope — decision: R3, not R2.** Pack §1 lists for R2 "trace engine, scope freezing, live diff" — the *diff* that shows out-of-scope

lot blocks the approval wrote (`recall_lot_blocks`), which R3 enforces at posting (exit block, KR-C). Delivering the revision before the
blocks are enforced would approve a delta whose effect cannot be tested. R2 records the findings (events + audit) so the R3 revision has its
input. Contract 1.1.0 records it as reconciliation R2-4; the state-machine line in the yaml now says R3.
