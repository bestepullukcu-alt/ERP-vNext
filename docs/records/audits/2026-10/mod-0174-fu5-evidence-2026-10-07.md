- **tr "parti"** (case-insensitive) in FU01 + the six Traceability families: 7 values, all in FU01, all meaning *lot*
  (`PageDescription`, `ColLot` ×2, `FilterLot` ×2, `ErrLotRequired` ×2). The `Partial*` hits are key NAMES (correction
  "yarım kaldı" texts), not values. Traceability tr: 0.
- **ar `دفع`** (covers `الدفعة`, `دفعة`, `دفعات`, `بالدفعات` and also `الدفع` = payment): 15 values, all in FU01, all
  meaning *lot*; none in the payment sense. Traceability ar: 0.
- **ru `продукт`**: 8 values, all `StockMovementsIndex.ru`. Not changed: `SkuLevelFinishedGood` = "Готовая продукция"
  (finished goods, an SKU level — another concept; `продукция` ≠ `продукт`).
- **Movement types** (`StockMovementsIndex.Movement{CODE}` ↔ `LotMovementsIndex.Movement.{CODE}`, 15 codes): en 5
  differ, tr 4 differ, **fr 4 differ** (only the apostrophe `'` vs `’` — found by the new test, P5 had reported fr as
  aligned), es / zh / ar / ru identical.
- **History field names** written by the service (production code): `status` (ChangeLotStatusHandler, LotExpirySweeper),
  `manufacturerLot`, `supplierLot`, `countryOfOrigin`, `coaReference`, `manufacturingDate`, `expiryDate`, `retestDate`
  (UpdateLotHandler), `typedText` (CommitManualLotMigrationHandler), `genealogy.parent` (LinkGenealogyHandler).
  Dates arrive as wire datetimes (`TraceMapping.Wire`), statuses as `LotStatus` names, the country as ISO alpha-2.
- **User-name resolution in Web: none shared.** The only resolver is private to
  `Controllers/CRM/ConsentPreferencesController.cs` (`ResolveUserNamesAsync`, one AuthService `/api/users/{id}` call per
  id, gated by `auth.users.read`, falls back to the GUID) — not a component, outside the allowed paths, and it would
  need a permission the trace roles do not carry. **The actor stays the user id** → *FU-LOC2-04 together: needs a
  central user directory.* No user lookup API written. (`LotHistoryEntryDto.ActorDisplay` exists in the contract; the
  service never fills it.)
- **F-2**: P3's pattern = `CssClass = "min-tablet-l"` on `Col.Status` and `Col.OnHand` in `_DataTable.cshtml`; Expiry
  had none.
